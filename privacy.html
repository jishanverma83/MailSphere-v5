Privacy Policy

Introduction

MailSphere S, created by Jishan Verma, helps school teams organize Gmail communication. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

Information We Access

After you give permission, MailSphere S may access your Google profile name, Gmail address, profile picture, and Gmail messages needed to provide inbox and analytics features. Passwords are never stored.

How We Use Gmail Data

Gmail data is used only to display, categorize, prioritize, and analyze your messages inside MailSphere S. Gmail access happens only after user permission. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Google OAuth Permissions

MailSphere S uses Google OAuth and requests read-only Gmail access together with basic profile information. Google handles authentication and does not share your password with MailSphere S.

Data Storage

Access tokens and profile details may be kept in your browser to maintain your signed-in session. Email and demo data may also be stored locally in your browser. We do not operate a separate server database for your Gmail content.

User Rights

You may review, update, or remove locally stored application data through your browser settings and the app controls. You may stop using the service at any time.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke access anytime from their Google Account. Signing out also removes the app's local authentication cache and revokes the active Google access token when available.

Contact Information

For privacy questions, contact jishanverma43@gmail.com. MailSphere S was created by Jishan Verma.

Privacy Policy

How MailSphere handles your school email data.

MailSphere uses Google OAuth to connect to the Gmail account you choose. Access is read-only and is used to display and organize email messages inside the application.

Information we use

The app may process your Google profile details, message metadata, message content, categories, and local preferences to provide inbox, analytics, alert, and school intelligence features.

Storage and security

Gmail access tokens and cached email data are stored in your browser for the current app experience. MailSphere does not sell your data or modify Gmail messages. AI requests should use a server-side proxy and must not expose provider API keys in browser code.

Your control

You can sign out at any time. Sign out revokes the Google token, clears browser storage, resets the local email cache, and returns you to the sign-in page. You can also clear demo data from Settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to jishanverma43@gmail.com.
