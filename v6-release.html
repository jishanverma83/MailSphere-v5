MailSphere V6

Release notes · School email intelligence, with the V5 foundation intact.

Reader AI

Quick Reply, Summarize, Translate, Explain Simply, and Draft Reply now open from a floating reader action. Results are editable before Gmail compose opens.

School Intelligence

Homework, fees, exams, circulars, leave, emergency, and transport signals are derived into dashboard cards and a clickable smart timeline.

Reliability

Sign out revokes the OAuth token, clears browser caches and the in-memory store, disables automatic account selection, and survives refreshes on localhost and Vercel.

AI deployment note

Set a server-side window.MAILSPHERE_RUNTIME.geminiEndpoint proxy for Gemini. API keys are intentionally never stored in client code; local fallback drafts keep the reader usable when offline.
